# Action-Oriented Project (AoP) Lifecycle

- From AI brainstorming to replication - turning ideas into lasting impact.
- An Action-Oriented Project is an event embedded inside a project, rather than a project embedded inside an event.

---

## 1. AI Brainstorming & Visioning

- Start the conversation
- Articulate the vision using AI.
- Explore ideas, ask tough questions.
- Refine thinking through dialogue.
- Publish the conversations (your digital footprint).
- **Output:** Clearer vision, initial concepts, public AI conversations.

---

## 2. Public Preparation

- Read → Think → Contribute
- Make project materials public (goals, background, existing work).
- People study, respond and add their ideas.
- Use structured email and collaboration tools.
- **Output:** Growing community, contributions, questions, early groups.

---

## 3. Asynchronous Planning

- Organize & Align
- Contributions are triaged by experienced editors.
- Weekly digest and task list are created.
- Groups form around tasks and interests.
- **Output:** Clear plan, task groups, pre-event momentum.

---

## 4. The AoP Gathering

- Midway Acceleration Point
- Review progress and insights.
- Brainstorm and solve problems.
- Make decisions.
- Take collective action.
- **Output:** Decisions, commitments, next steps, energized teams.

---

## 5. Action & Execution

- Turn Plans into Results
- Teams work on real project tasks.
- Create research, documents, prototypes, campaigns, etc.
- Share progress publicly.
- Track work and results.
- **Output:** Tangible outputs, visible progress, growing impact.

---

## 6. Ongoing Project

- Sustain & Grow
- Continue collaboration online.
- Keep the website as a living project dossier (goals, tasks, outputs, decisions, logs).
- Measure impact and share results.
- Invite new contributors.
- **Output:** A living project with measurable progress and a growing team.

---

## 7. Replication

- Create More Missions
- Participants gain the skills and confidence to start their own projects.
- Share the model, tools and learnings.
- New AoPs emerge, creating a multiplier effect.
- **Output:** New projects, new leaders, new communities, greater impact.

---

## The AoP Mindset

- It's a project, not just an event.
- It starts before the event and continues after.
- Everyone can contribute, grow and lead.
- Outputs matter more than attendance.
- It's designed to be replicated.

---

## Key Principles

- Open & Public
- Distributed & Inclusive
- Action & Impact
- Continuous & Replicable

---

## The Result

- Real solutions.
- Stronger communities.
- A healthier planet.
- Because when people work together on the right things, good things happen.
