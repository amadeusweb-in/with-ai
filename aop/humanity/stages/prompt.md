I suggest this common heading structure:

```text
# [Title]

## Purpose

## The Proposition

## Why It Matters

## What It Means

## What It Could Look Like

## Questions

## Invitations

## Possible Actions

## Evidence

## Related Conversations

## Replies
```

### Why these headings?

- **Purpose** — establishes what the file is doing.
- **The Proposition** — the core idea someone can agree/disagree with.
- **Why It Matters** — gives the rationale.
- **What It Means** — removes ambiguity.
- **What It Could Look Like** — moves from abstraction toward examples.
- **Questions** — explicitly creates things people can reply to.
- **Invitations** — asks people to contribute thinking.
- **Possible Actions** — converts thinking into AoP activity.
- **Evidence** — separates aspiration from what we actually know.
- **Related Conversations** — links to AI chats, presentations, etc.
- **Replies** — provides a natural place for responses to accumulate.

I particularly like **The Proposition** because it changes the character of the document. It says:

> *Here is something we are putting into the world for you to respond to.*

rather than:

> *Here is an essay we expect you to read.*
