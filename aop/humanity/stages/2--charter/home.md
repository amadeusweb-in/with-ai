## 2) Charter

### Purpose**
- Define the shared charter underlying the Humanity AoP.

### About**
- Connect the Humanity vision to the 15 DAWN principles without implementing the principles individually.

### Prompt**

- Write a concise shared charter for human flourishing.
- Explain why a common charter is useful for collective action.
- Connect the charter to the 15 DAWN principles.
- Emphasize unity, diversity, justice, care and long-term thinking.
- Keep it self-contained and suitable for public publication.
