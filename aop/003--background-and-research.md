### The distinction

**Full-frontal intellectual assault:**

> Here is the diagnosis of humanity.  
> Here is why existing systems are broken.  
> Here is the philosophical alternative.  
> Now convince people.

**Action-oriented project:**

> Here is one small piece of the future we want.  
> Let's build it.  
> Let's invite people into it.  
> Let's see what happens.  
> Let's learn.  
> Let's improve it.  
> Then show everyone what happened.
