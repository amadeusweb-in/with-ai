## Action-Oriented Project - TL;DR

> **An Action-Oriented Project is a public, ongoing project that uses a gathering to accelerate real work - rather than an event that happens to contain a project.** ([AmadeusWeb](https://amadeusweb.in/aop/how-it-works/presentation/?embed=1))

**You don't come to an AoP primarily to attend.  
You come to contribute.**

Before the gathering, people can:

- read the mission and existing work
- ask questions
- research
- share knowledge and ideas
- find other people
- take on activities
- start projects
- prepare things to be decided or built

The **gathering is the acceleration point**: people review what has happened, resolve questions, make decisions and commit to action. ([AmadeusWeb](https://amadeusweb.in/aop/005--definitions-and-scaffolding/))

Then **the project continues**.

People produce things. Actions create evidence. Projects evolve. New people take ownership. And eventually participants should be able to **start or replicate projects of their own**. ([AmadeusWeb](https://amadeusweb.in/aop/005--definitions-and-scaffolding/))

### In one line

**Mission → People → Activities → Projects → Action → Evidence → Replication**

### The really short version

> **Don't organise an event about the work.  
> Organise the work, and let the event accelerate it.**

That's the AoP.
