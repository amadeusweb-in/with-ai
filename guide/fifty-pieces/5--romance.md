## V. ROMANCE - love is part of human flourishing

### 37. **R-13. Sleep Eros - 22 Aug 2015**
**Proposition:** Intimacy is more than sexual access; companionship and knowing one another matter.

**Practice:** Let relationship develop before possession.

**DAWN implication:** Don't turn human beings into consumable experiences.

### 38. **158. Longing - 16 Sep 2019**
**Proposition:** Longing itself can reveal the depth of attachment.

**Practice:** Don't automatically suppress desire; understand what it reveals.

**DAWN implication:** Desire can be material for self-knowledge.

### 39. **162. Ark - 22 Sep 2019**
**Proposition:** Two people can choose to construct a life of abundance together.

**Practice:** Build, don't merely dream.

**DAWN implication:** Romance can become **co-creation**.

### 40. **179. Niyat - 16 Dec 2019**
**Proposition:** Intention matters.

**Practice:** Examine the quality behind your action.

**DAWN implication:** The same outward action can carry radically different inner qualities.

### 41. **411. Mademoiselle2 - 21 Apr 2022**
**Proposition:** Romantic imagination remains part of the human being even amid a much larger philosophical project.

**Practice:** Don't sterilise the human person in pursuit of an abstract ideal.

**DAWN implication:** A future civilisation still needs **beauty, attraction, tenderness and play**.

### 42. **414. All My Life - 24 Apr 2022**
**Proposition:** The imagined beloved is someone who wants to delight the world's children with you.

**Practice:** Let intimate love expand into shared service.

**DAWN implication:** **The beloved isn't an escape from the world.**

### 43. **483. Beach - 15 Dec 2022**
**Proposition:** Passion and joy are legitimate dimensions of life.

**Practice:** Don't mistake spiritual seriousness for joylessness.

**DAWN implication:** A civilisation of consciousness should still know how to **laugh, play and desire**.

### 44. **520. Elated - 2 Feb 2024**
**Proposition:** The romantic and ecstatic currents remain alive even after the larger project has matured.

**Practice:** Remain capable of wonder.

**DAWN implication:** Don't build a movement that forgets the individual heart.
