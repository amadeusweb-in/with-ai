## III. NATURE - Prakriti is not background scenery

### 20. **15. Nature - 22 Jan 2017**
**Proposition:** Love must extend beyond humans to plants and animals.

**Practice:** Develop affection for the living world.

**DAWN implication:** A human-centred civilisation is insufficient.

### 21. **24. Acres Wild - 19 Mar 2017**
**Proposition:** Wild places restore something civilisation suppresses.

**Practice:** Preserve contact with wonder and untamed nature.

**DAWN implication:** Nature isn't merely a resource or recreational backdrop.

### 22. **178. Mountains - 13 Dec 2019**
**Proposition:** Landscape can become part of the spiritual imagination.

**Practice:** Let Nature teach.

**DAWN implication:** The Tolkien connection matters here: wilderness can be a place where another conception of humanity becomes imaginable.

### 23. **259. Dear Mother - 10 Nov 2020**
**Proposition:** Ask what Mother Earth would have us become.

**Practice:** Reverse the question from *“What can Earth give us?”* to *“What does Earth ask of us?”*

**DAWN implication:** That's very close to your Maa Shakti language.

### 24. **306. Farm Visitors - 14 Feb 2021**
**Proposition:** Contact with living Nature can restore perspective lost in urban life.

**Practice:** Go where life is actually happening.

**DAWN implication:** DAWN should not become another entirely virtual civilisation.

### 25. **324. Skandan - 22 Mar 2021**
**Proposition:** Beauty, trees, growth and seasonality belong to the texture of conscious life.

**Practice:** Notice.

**DAWN implication:** **Attention itself can be ecological practice.**

### 26. **505. Exodus - 1 Jun 2023**
**Proposition:** Humanity may need an actual exodus from destructive patterns of urban civilisation toward living with Nature.

**Practice:** Experiment with alternative ways of inhabiting the Earth.

**DAWN implication:** This is potentially one of DAWN's most radical ideas: **not merely improving the existing system, but creating places where another pattern of life can be tried.**
