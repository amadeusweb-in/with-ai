## VI. RELATIONSHIPS - the philosophy has to work between people

### 45. **166. Promise2 - 14 Oct 2019**
**Proposition:** Friendship means remaining alongside someone through difficulty.

**Practice:** Be dependable.

**DAWN implication:** A network isn't a network because people have accounts. **It becomes a network when people show up for one another.**

### 46. **173. Heal - 25 Nov 2019**
**Proposition:** Healing requires releasing destructive patterns of thought.

**Practice:** Help one another recover rather than perpetually reopening wounds.

**DAWN implication:** DAWN needs a **culture of repair**.

### 47. **241. Candles in the Wind - 28 Aug 2020**

**Proposition:** A person's contribution survives through the joy and goodness they leave in others.

**Practice:** Live in a way that leaves warmth behind.

**DAWN implication:** Legacy is relational, not merely institutional.

### 48. **316. Hands - 7 Mar 2021**

**Proposition:** We need someone willing to walk beside us.

**Practice:** Offer companionship.

**DAWN implication:** No grand theory compensates for loneliness.

### 49. **319. No Quit - 15 Mar 2021**

**Proposition:** People sometimes need another person's strength when their own is depleted.

**Practice:** Encourage perseverance without pretending suffering isn't real.

**DAWN implication:** **Mutual resilience** is a social technology.

### 50. **481. Sorry - 14 Dec 2022**

**Proposition:** Sometimes the right response to another person is simply to acknowledge one's own failure.

**Practice:** Say sorry.

**DAWN implication:** This tiny principle may be more important than half the grand metaphysics.
