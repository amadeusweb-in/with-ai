## II. RELIGION - transcend the tribal boundary

### 10. **I-1. Religious Appeal - 14 Feb 2005**
**Proposition:** The great religious teachers need not become banners under which human beings divide themselves.

**Practice:** Seek the common spiritual substance beneath traditions.

**DAWN implication:** **No religious tribalism.**

This is one of the earliest pieces in the canon and therefore especially important: the pluralist instinct predates the later DAWN architecture by decades.

### 11. **I-5. Reconciling Quality - 13 Aug 2013**
**Proposition:** Living individuals cannot simply be forced into static categories.

**Practice:** Reconcile inherited truth with living experience.

**DAWN implication:** **Tradition must remain alive enough to meet the person standing before it.**

### 12. **I-7. Harmony - Nov 2015**
**Proposition:** Different paths need not cancel each other.

**Practice:** Embrace brethren while acknowledging one's own limitations.

**DAWN implication:** Don't confuse conviction with exclusivity.

### 13. **R-14. Quality - 21 Nov 2015**
**Proposition:** Judge people by the quality of their humanity, not by religious identity.

**Practice:** Let actual character outrank labels.

**DAWN implication:** A person's tradition is context-not a moral scorecard.

### 14. **R-15. Divine - 22 Nov 2015**
**Proposition:** The Divine can be contemplated through many attributes and through the complementary powers represented by Mahakala and Mahashakti.

**Practice:** Approach the Divine with breadth rather than reducing it to one mental image.

**DAWN implication:** This is one of the texts that deserves particular attention alongside your present **Maa Shakti / Prakriti** formulation.

### 15. **I-12. Religion and Islam - Sep 2017**
**Proposition:** Engage a living religion seriously rather than treating it as an abstract enemy.

**Practice:** Understand before judging.

**DAWN implication:** Religious literacy becomes part of peace-making.

### 16. **I-13. Our Values - 1 Mar 2018**
**Proposition:** Spiritual aspiration has institutional consequences.

**Practice:** Service, truth, openness, freedom, grievance redressal, courtesy, peace, hospitality, education, responsibility and upliftment.

**DAWN implication:** **If your values don't survive contact with administration, they're not yet values.**

This is one of the most important DAWN texts because it begins translating aspiration into social behaviour.

### 17. **I-21. God Again - 14 Sep 2019**
**Proposition:** God is not merely an abstraction but a living, loving, all-pervading Spirit, with a Divine Spark within each person.

**Practice:** Allow for evolution and change without losing the Divine centre.

**DAWN implication:** Spiritual evolution should make us **more alive**, not more dogmatic.

### 18. **I-22. God In India - 15 Sep 2019**
**Proposition:** India can be contemplated not merely politically or culturally but spiritually.

**Practice:** Understand India's civilisational experiment in its deeper dimensions.

**DAWN implication:** This connects directly with your 2003 decision to remain in India-not as chauvinism, but as a conviction about India's role in the human experiment.

### 19. **I-23. Jump with Faith - 25 Dec 2019**
**Proposition:** Religion can become a battlefield, but humanity can transform the battlefield into an orchestra.

**Practice:** Faith accompanied by liberty, brotherhood and harmony.

**DAWN implication:** **Faith should enlarge human possibility, not narrow it.**
