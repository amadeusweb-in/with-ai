## IV. SOCIETY - participate in evolution

### 27. **I-2. Tripping - 22 Feb 2005**
**Proposition:** India possesses enormous human potential but needs people willing to accept responsibility and change dysfunctional patterns.

**Practice:** Don't merely criticise society-participate in repairing it.

**DAWN implication:** This is the **2005 ancestor of DAWN**.

### 28. **I-4. Dear Brother - 10 Aug 2013**
**Proposition:** The apparent opposing camps of humanity ultimately belong to one family.

**Practice:** Work toward reconciliation rather than victory over the other.

**DAWN implication:** **This is the central text.**

The goal isn't to make one side win.

It's to discover that there were never ultimately two sides.

### 29. **I-6. Charter - 14 Aug 2015**
**Proposition:** The vision needs a framework through which people can cooperate.

**Practice:** Translate ideals into shared commitments.

**DAWN implication:** **Vision without structure evaporates. Structure without vision becomes bureaucracy.**

### 30. **83. Love Our Nature - 26 Oct 2018**
**Proposition:** Humanity belongs to one world.

**Practice:** Reject war and cultivate planetary belonging.

**DAWN implication:** A DAWN inhabitant should be capable of thinking beyond nation, tribe and faction.

### 31. **111. Enough - 12 Apr 2019**
**Proposition:** There comes a point when passive acceptance becomes complicity.

**Practice:** Recognise when “enough” has been reached.

**DAWN implication:** Peace isn't passivity.

### 32. **112. Fight2 - 12 Apr 2019**
**Proposition:** Social liberation sometimes requires struggle.

**Practice:** Fight *for* something rather than merely against something.

**DAWN implication:** **Non-complacency without hatred.**

### 33. **127. Humanity - 2 May 2019**
**Proposition:** A peaceful world requires us to recover our humanity.

**Practice:** Become human before trying to engineer humanity.

**DAWN implication:** This may be the simplest statement of the entire project.

### 34. **L-1. Cry Liberty - 16 Feb 2020**
**Proposition:** Freedom belongs to humanity as a whole.

**Practice:** Resist systems that unnecessarily imprison human possibility.

**DAWN implication:** Freedom isn't a luxury reserved for the technically or economically privileged.

### 35. **256. Cry Peace - 2 Nov 2020**
**Proposition:** Peace needs to be actively demanded.

**Practice:** Make peace a positive project.

**DAWN implication:** **Peace is something people build.**

### 36. **300. Country Roads - 25 Jan 2021**
**Proposition:** Technology, transparency, goodwill and sincerity can be brought together for public benefit.

**Practice:** Build practical infrastructure that people can trust.

**DAWN implication:** This is where **your technologist and humanist finally shake hands**.
